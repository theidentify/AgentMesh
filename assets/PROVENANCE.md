# AgentMesh artwork provenance

The SVG geometry, custom vector letterforms, and ASCII mark in this directory
were authored specifically for AgentMesh. They were not traced or extracted
from another logo, stock asset, font, icon library, or agent/vendor brand.

## Assets

- `agentmesh-icon.svg`: square connected-peer icon.
- `agentmesh-icon-mono.svg`: monochrome mark.
- `agentmesh-logo.svg`: transparent wordmark for light surfaces.
- `agentmesh-logo-dark.svg`: transparent wordmark for dark surfaces.
- Terminal equivalent: `[o-A-o]` and the three-peer ASCII banner in `brand.py`.

## Dependency and license boundary

webfont, imported icon pack, or third-party artwork. The lettering is SVG path
geometry, not an outlined copy of a font. No third-party asset license is
